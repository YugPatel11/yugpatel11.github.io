The Problem & Why It Matters

The gap between planning at home and surviving on the ground

The Fragmentation

A traveler typically juggles 5+ disconnected apps: one for planning, one for translation, one for maps, one for emergencies, and one for train or bus bookings.

The Ground Reality Gap

Travelers spend hours planning at home, but get stranded on the ground. Generic planners fail because they do not handle real-world chaos: language barriers, medical emergencies, or schedule disruptions.

The India Factor

22 official languages, unpredictable weather, and unorganized local markets make standard Western AI planners unreliable for Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian destinations.

The Proposed Solution

All-in-one, resilient, India-first smart travel companion

All-in-One India Companion

A single unified platform that not only generates slot-by-slot AI itineraries but stays actively with the user on the ground through every leg of the journey.

Multi-City Routing

Automatically plans inter-connected trips across neighboring cities (e.g., Indore → Ujjain → Omkareshwar) with integrated train/bus timings and transit route optimization.

Unfair Resilience Advantage

Multi-provider AI fallback (Gemini → Groq Llama → OpenRouter) combined with an Offline Rule-Based Engine and 509K+ BharatPOI dataset so itineraries generate even with zero network.

Key Features

Core intelligent capabilities powering Ghumify

AI-Powered Personalized Planning

Generates customized, slot-by-slot travel itineraries tailored to destination, available hours, party age group, pacing, and interest categories.

Smart Route Optimization

Uses Haversine proximity calculations and Nearest-Neighbor logic to plan efficient, loop-free routes and anchored lunch/dinner schedules.

Crowd Intelligence

Machine learning predicts peak crowd congestion windows at attractions and dynamically suggests calmer alternatives and off-peak visiting slots.

Budget Intelligence

Accurately estimates overall trip expenses and includes integer-paise math expense settlement algorithms to eliminate group debt rounding errors.

Multilingual Translator

Built-in two-way translation NLP supporting 10 major Indian languages (Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Odia).

Offline Travel Planning

Offline deterministic rule-based engine coupled with a bundled 509K+ BharatPOI dataset and SQLite local storage to generate itineraries without internet.

Multi-City Travel

Seamless interconnected routing across neighboring Indian cities with synchronized inter-city train and bus options and realistic adult/child fares.

Emergency Assistance (SOS)

1-touch SOS emergency dialing (112, 100, 108) and real-time OpenStreetMap Overpass discovery of nearby hospitals, pharmacies, police stations, and verified restrooms.

Real-Time Adaptation

Automatically recalibrates schedules in response to weather shifts, unexpected venue closures, or in-flight user modifications through conversational AI.

Dietary & Accessibility Support

Strict Vegetarian & Jain dietary safety locks, automatic removal of nightlife spots for families with kids, and wheelchair/elderly accessibility checks.
